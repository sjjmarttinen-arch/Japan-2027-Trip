Tue Mar 2 • Kyoto

25th Anniversary & Kyoto Watches

Wood-fired sourdough pizza & seasonal vegetables at Restaurant Monk on the Philosopher’s Path, plus commemorative watches at KUOE Kyoto & Maker’s Watch Knot.

Mar 6 & Mar 11 • Fuji & Tokyo

Kawaguchiko Fishing & Tackle Shops

Guided morning bass and trout charter on Lake Kawaguchiko, followed by Tokyo’s legendary Joshuya, Sansui & Tackle Berry stores.

Osaka • Kawaguchiko • Tokyo

Vintage, Retro Games & Craft Beer

Treasure hunting in Amerikamura, Hard Off Kawaguchiko, Nakano Broadway & Shimokitazawa, plus Minoh Beer, Fujizakura Heights & Watering Hole.

Mar 1, 4, 6 & 9 • Osaka, Fuji & Tokyo

Custom Eyeglasses, Cosmetics & Cafés

Fast 30–60 min custom glasses at JINS, Zoff or OWNDAYS, lakeside boutiques at Fuji Oishi Hanaterrace, and Harajuku’s 3-story @cosme TOKYO.

Wed Mar 3 • Sanyo Shinkansen

Himeji Castle & Hiroshima Peace Park

Bullet train excursion to Himeji Castle (“White Heron Castle”), layered okonomiyaki at Okonomimura, the Peace Park, and Hiroshima Orizuru Tower.
